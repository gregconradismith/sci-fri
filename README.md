# Science Friday

Printable math activities for ages 5–14, designed for 10–20 minute drop-in visits: coloring and symmetry, paper folding, strategy games, Conway's Game of Life, orbifolds, and mathematical puzzles.

## Start here

Browse [the website](https://gregconradismith.github.io/sci-fri/), or download and unzip this repository, then open [00-START-HERE.html](00-START-HERE.html) in a browser. The illustrated catalog includes suggested ages, supplies, exact print ranges, original source links, and a six-session plan. Local PDFs and previews work offline.

- [Quick Stations](00-Quick-Stations-Ages-5-14.pdf): setup and printing plan, four station signs with three levels, worksheets, and leader notes.
- [Starter Pack](00-Science-Friday-Starter-Pack.pdf): ten pages of original coloring, games, Game of Life grids, and answers.
- [Text guide](START-HERE.txt): a quick overview and starter-pack page guide.

The collection contains 21 hosted PDFs and 191 pages, plus seven Maths Craft NZ resources linked at their original source, including teacher material. For a first session, print starter-pack pages 1 and 5 plus the cube net in `02-Polyhedra-and-Folding`. Bring colored pencils, paper, scissors, and glue sticks or tape.

## Sources and permissions

Downloaded resources retain their original content and attribution. The catalog and `_sources` manifests identify their original sources, including JRMF, Mathigon, Maths Craft NZ, Math for Love, and individual authors. Third-party materials remain subject to their respective authors' terms; this repository does not grant a blanket license to those materials.

## Editable material

Python scripts in `_sources` generate the original worksheet packs and catalog. Some scripts depend on PDF/image libraries and local rendering tools; the catalog builder uses page renders in `_build/qa`. Temporary renders and extraction files in `_build` are excluded from Git. The supplied PDFs and HTML can be used without running the builders.

See [_sources/VERIFICATION.txt](_sources/VERIFICATION.txt) for the existing collection checks and [WHATS-NEW.txt](WHATS-NEW.txt) for the October 1, 2026 expansion.
