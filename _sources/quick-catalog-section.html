Print p. 1 for setup, pp. 2–5 as table signs, pp. 6–8 as activity sheets, and p. 9 for the leader. The signs offer starting points for ages 5–7, 8–10, and 11–14. This is original material you can adapt.

First print batch: 20 art sheets; 10 cube/tetrahedron nets; 10 sheets cut into 50 long strips; 8 reusable game sheets; 8 reusable puzzle sheets; 4 station signs. Keep spare paper and adjust quantities after seeing which stations fill up. Print detailed teacher guides once per table.

Staffing: one helper at the building station and another available for games and puzzles is a useful starting arrangement. Adjust to the age mix and available adults. Precut pieces and prepare a finished model before the event.
